## Risk Communication Strategy

### Risk Categories and Communication Approach

#### High-Risk Issues

- **Criteria**: Project timeline impact >2 weeks, budget impact >10%, stakeholder satisfaction risk
- **Communication**: Immediate notification to executives, daily updates until resolved
- **Channels**: Direct email, emergency meetings, dashboard alerts
- **Timeline**: Within 2 hours of identification

#### Medium-Risk Issues

- **Criteria**: Project timeline impact 1-2 weeks, budget impact 5-10%, quality concerns
- **Communication**: Next scheduled executive update, increased monitoring
- **Channels**: Weekly reports, stakeholder meetings
- **Timeline**: Within 24 hours of identification

#### Low-Risk Issues

- **Criteria**: Minor timeline impact, minimal budget impact, easily resolved
- **Communication**: Regular reporting channels, team-level communication
- **Channels**: Team meetings, regular reports
- **Timeline**: Next scheduled communication

### Crisis Communication Plan

#### Crisis Definition

- Project failure scenarios that threaten business objectives
- Major security breaches or compliance violations
- Significant budget overruns or timeline delays
- Critical stakeholder relationship issues

#### Crisis Communication Protocol

1. **Immediate Response** (0-2 hours):
  - Notify crisis communication team
  - Assess impact and gather facts
  - Prepare initial communication
  - Notify key stakeholders

2. **Short-term Response** (2-24 hours):
  - Detailed impact assessment
  - Recovery plan development
  - Stakeholder meetings
  - Media/external communication if needed

3. **Long-term Response** (1+ days):
  - Regular progress updates
  - Recovery plan execution
  - Lessons learned capture
  - Relationship repair activities
